Who we are

About CyberVault

CyberVault IT Centre University prepares curious, capable students to shape a safer and more connected digital future.

Our history

CyberVault began as a community technology centre created to make practical digital skills accessible to more people. As demand for skilled technology professionals grew, the centre expanded into a university focused on applied learning, research, and service to the community. Today, students learn through a blend of strong academic foundations and hands-on projects.

Our mission

To provide inclusive, practical, and high-quality technology education that equips graduates to solve real problems with integrity.

Our vision

To be a trusted centre of learning and innovation, helping communities thrive in a secure digital world.
